## 2024-05-18 - Debouncing Canvas Resize Animations\n**Learning:** When debouncing canvas array initializations on window resize, updating `canvas.width` and `canvas.height` must happen immediately and outside the debounce block to prevent the canvas from stretching or distorting during the resize process, while only the expensive array reallocation is debounced.\n**Action:** Always decouple immediate canvas dimension updates from expensive, debounced rendering re-initializations on `resize` events.
